Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026.

§ Who we are

RecruitCloud LLC (“RecruitCloud,” “we,” “us”) is a Delaware limited liability company with offices in Miami Beach, Florida. We build AI sourcing systems for recruiting teams and staffing agencies, and we provide candidate sourcing and outreach services.

This policy covers two groups of people: (1) visitors to recruitcloud.io and the people who work with us as clients or prospects, and (2) professionals and job candidates whose information we process when we source candidates for our clients. If you received a recruiting message from us, Sections 3 to 6 explain where your information came from and how to remove it.

§ 1. Information from visitors, clients and prospects
  • Contact and business details you give us: name, work email, phone, company, job title, and anything you send in a message or booking form
  • Account and project information our clients share with us to set up their sourcing systems, such as job descriptions and details about past hires
  • Website usage data: IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed and referring site
  • Business contact information about prospective clients from public sources and data providers, used for business development outreach
§ 2. How we use visitor and client information
  • To respond to inquiries, schedule calls and provide our services
  • To build, run and support the sourcing systems we deliver
  • To send business communications about our services, which you can opt out of at any time
  • To bill, keep records, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations
  • To operate, secure and improve our website
§ 3. Candidate information: what we collect and where it comes from

To find candidates for our clients' open roles, we collect professional information about people who may be a fit:

  • Name, current and past job titles, employers, work history, skills, education and general location
  • Professional contact details, such as work or personal email addresses and phone numbers
  • Links to public professional profiles and online work, such as LinkedIn or GitHub
  • Our communications with you, including whether you replied or asked us to stop contacting you

We get this information from publicly available professional sources on the internet, from professional data and contact-enrichment providers, from our clients, and from you. We do not seek sensitive information such as health, religion, immigration status or background-check data, and we do not use protected characteristics to select or rank candidates.

§ 4. How we use candidate information
  • To identify people whose experience matches a client's open role
  • To contact you about relevant job opportunities, by email, phone or text where the law allows
  • To share your professional profile with a hiring client when you may be a fit or have expressed interest
  • To keep a do-not-contact list so we honor opt-out requests

AI and automated tools. We use AI and automated software to search for, match and rank professional profiles and to help draft outreach messages. People on our team and our clients review the results. We do not use automated tools to make hiring decisions or any decision with a legal or similarly significant effect on you; hiring decisions are made by our clients.

Legal basis (EU/UK). Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on our and our clients' legitimate interest in connecting qualified professionals with relevant job opportunities. You can object at any time, and we will stop.

§ 5. Who we share information with
  • Hiring clients, who receive candidate profiles for roles they are filling
  • Service providers that work on our behalf, such as cloud hosting, databases, email and messaging delivery, CRM and data-enrichment providers, under contracts that limit their use of the data
  • Professional advisors and authorities, when required by law or to protect our rights
  • A buyer or successor if our business is sold or merged

We do not sell personal information for money. Providing candidate profiles to hiring clients may count as a “sale” or “sharing” under some state privacy laws. You can opt out of that at any time using the steps in Section 7.

§ 6. Email and text outreach

Every outreach email we send identifies RecruitCloud and includes a way to opt out. We honor email opt-outs within 10 business days and text opt-outs (reply STOP) promptly. Once you opt out, we add you to a suppression list so you are not contacted again, including by systems we run for clients.

§ 7. Your rights and how to remove your data

Depending on where you live, including California, other U.S. states with privacy laws, the EU and the UK, you may have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we have about you, where it came from and who we shared it with, and get a copy
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your information
  • Opt out of any sale or sharing, and object to our use of your information
  • Not be treated differently for using these rights

To use these rights, visit Remove my data or email isaac@recruitcloud.io with “Privacy request” in the subject. We will confirm receipt within 10 business days and respond within 45 days. We may ask you to confirm your identity. An authorized agent may make a request for you with your signed permission. If you are in the EU or UK and are not satisfied, you may complain to your local data protection authority.

§ 8. Data retention

We keep candidate information for up to 24 months after our last interaction with you, unless you become a client's applicant or employee or ask us to delete it sooner. We keep client and business records for as long as needed for our work and legal obligations. We keep a minimal record of opt-out requests indefinitely so we can honor them.

§ 9. Security

We protect personal information with encryption in transit, access controls and two-factor login on our business systems, and by limiting access to people who need it. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your information, we will notify you as the law requires.

§ 10. Cookies and tracking

Our website uses only the cookies needed to run it and the services embedded on it, such as our scheduling tool. We do not use advertising trackers on recruitcloud.io. We honor Global Privacy Control signals. If we add analytics or advertising tools, we will update this policy first.

§ 11. International transfers

We are based in the United States, and information is stored and processed in the U.S. Where the law requires, we use appropriate safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses, for information transferred from the EU or UK.

§ 12. Children

Our services are for businesses and working professionals. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

§ 13. Changes to this policy

We will post any update here with a new “Last updated” date. If a change is material, we will give notice on our website before it takes effect.
