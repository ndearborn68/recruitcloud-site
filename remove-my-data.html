Remove my data

Last updated: November 7, 2026.

§ Opt out or delete your information

If we contacted you about a job opportunity or you want us to stop using your information, we will remove you. You do not need an account.

email a removal request

Or email isaac@recruitcloud.io with “Remove my data” in the subject. Include every email address and phone number we may have contacted, so we can find all your records.

§ What happens next
  • We stop outreach within 10 business days, usually much sooner. Replying STOP to any text works immediately.
  • We confirm your request within 10 business days and complete it within 45 days.
  • We delete your profile and keep only your email or phone number on a do-not-contact list, so you are not contacted again, including by systems we run for clients.
  • You can also ask for a copy of the information we have about you, or ask us to correct it.
§ More information

See our Privacy Policy for what we collect, where it comes from and your rights.
